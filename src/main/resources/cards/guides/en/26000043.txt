note: Written on November 28, 2026. Hitpoints and other stats come from the in-game card info (level 16); how the card is used and its matchups come from this site's data on ranked battles of the top 1,000 players.

## What it does
The Elite Barbarians are a 6-Elixir card that deploys two troops. Their speed is Fast, and they attack ground troops and buildings.
Each has 2,143 hitpoints at level 16 and deals 613 damage every 1.4 seconds. At the same level, Rocket is the only spell that kills one in a single cast (Fireball leaves 1,043 and Lightning leaves 454).

## How to use it
They are fast and each one is tough, so after defending with them you can send them straight into a counterattack, forcing your opponent to spend a lot of Elixir.
Among the top 1,000 players, 70% of Elite Barbarians decks also run Zap, and about 40% run Heal Spirit, Giant and Rage. Decks of top players include a version with Giant, Dark Prince and Rage, and a version with Three Musketeers and Elixir Collector.

## How to counter it
- Defend with troops: only a Rocket kills one with a single spell. At the same level, Mini P.E.K.K.A and P.E.K.K.A need 2 hits and a Knight 7 to kill one.
- Attack from the air: they only hit ground targets, so they cannot fight back against flying troops such as Mega Minion.
- In battles between top players, decks with the Elite Barbarians win less often against decks with Inferno Tower, Tombstone, Barbarians or Suspicious Bush (see "weak against" below).

# Electro Giant. Written 2026-11-28 from cards/stats.tsv (level 16) and that day's production data (top 1,000 players).

note: Written on November 28, 2026. Hitpoints and other stats come from the in-game card info (level 16); how the card is used and its matchups come from this site's data on ranked battles of the top 1,000 players.

## What it does
The Electro Giant is a 7-Elixir troop that only targets buildings. It has 6,314 hitpoints at level 16 and its speed is Slow.
Troops that attack it from close range take 306 reflected damage and are stunned for 0.5 seconds. Even a Rocket of the same level leaves it with 3,942 hitpoints.

## How to use it
It is heavy and slow, so the usual plan is to push together with your other cards.
Among the top 1,000 players, more than 90% of Electro Giant decks also run Tornado, Lightning and Barbarian Barrel, and more than 80% run Bowler and Baby Dragon. Decks of top players include a 4.4-average-Elixir version that adds Mother Witch and Goblin Hut to those five.
Tornado pulls enemy troops together, so the reflected damage and the splash from Bowler and Baby Dragon can take them out at once.

## How to counter it
- Use cards with big hits: at the same level, P.E.K.K.A needs 5 hits and Mini P.E.K.K.A 6 (a Knight would need 20).
- Pull it with a building: it only targets buildings, so a building buys you time to take it down.
- In battles between top players, decks with the Electro Giant win less often against decks with Sparky, P.E.K.K.A, Wizard or Dark Prince (see "weak against" below).
